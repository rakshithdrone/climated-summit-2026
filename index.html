About the summit

What is ClimatEd Summit 2026?

Climate change can feel like a distant, global issue. But the choices we make every day, what we eat, buy, use and discard, are deeply connected to the planet.

ClimatEd Summit 2026 brings together students from different schools to explore these connections through student voices, expert perspectives, interactive experiences and collaborative problem-solving. It is organised by the Grade 8 students of The Riverside School with aProCh, as part of Climate Week India ’26.

Agenda · 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM

How the morning unfolds

  1. Experience & provocation

    Opening conversation

    Is individual action enough to address climate change?
  2. Inspirational spark

    Expert perspectives

    Invited experts give short, thought-provoking inputs and take questions directly from students.

  3. Showcase of solutions

    Climate innovation challenge

    Mixed inter-school teams pick a climate problem, design an innovative solution together, and pitch it as a real sustainability idea.

  4. Workshops

    Interactive experiences

    Stations and student-led exhibitions across the campus.

  5. Commit

    One action per school

    Each participating school names one specific, achievable action to take back to its school community.

Our experts

Three practitioners who work on climate every day

Dr. Rutul Joshi

Professor, CEPT University · Program Chair, Master of Urban Planning

Head of the Doctoral Office at CEPT, where he has shaped urban planning teaching since 2006. His research covers sustainable urban mobility, Transit-Oriented Development and low-carbon urban policy reform. He is a leading voice in national and international debates on climate-responsive urban development.

Mr. Tejas Joshi

Founder, Space Elements

A sustainability expert with over three decades in corporate management and a lifelong commitment to environmental conservation. At Space Elements he leads integrated solutions in water resource planning, rainwater harvesting, groundwater conservation and environmental sustainability.

Mr. Ashish Agrawal

Founder, Bridge4Change Foundation · IRMA alumnus

Advocates mindful consumption as a simple, powerful path to personal well-being and climate action. He earlier founded Paryavaran Mitra, a Gandhi Ashram-based social enterprise empowering rag-picking communities through solid waste management. He has led workshops in India, the UK and the US, and wrote Demystified and Eco-Bliss.
